Step 1

Two players, one table

The Spreader sits on one side and the Curber on the other.

Each has a row of cards along their own edge, facing them.

Spreader's cards
Curber's cards
Step 2

Start the outbreak

Spreader: put a flat piece on a busy street.

The people nearest it catch the virus, and the three-minute clock starts.

Step 3

Watch it spread

People pass it on when they are close to each other outdoors.

Glowing squares show where it is.

Step 4

Lock it down

Curber: put a cup on the map.

That zone is locked down. Its streets close and most people there stay in.

Lockdown: 12 points
Step 5

Play your cards

Hold a hand on a card for a second to buy it. Both sides earn points as the round goes on.

Curber: masks, testing, vaccination.
Spreader: a faster, further-reaching, harder-to-find virus.

Mask mandate bought
Spreads faster bought
On the screens

The room follows along

The TVs show the same round in 3D, with both players' cards the right way up.

Step 6

Time's up

After three minutes the table shows how far the virus got.

Hold a hand on any card to play again.
